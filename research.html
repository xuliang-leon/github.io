RESEARCH / EXPLORATION & IMPACT

Research

I explore how intelligent algorithms, incentives, and human behavior shape decisions—with applications in business, operations, and healthcare.

01 /

Human–Algorithm Decision-Making

When should decision-makers trust algorithms? How do recommendations influence learning, judgment, and performance? I study human interaction with analytical and AI-based decision support in operational settings.

Human–AI collaborationAlgorithmic decision supportLearning and feedback

Related work: Replenishment recommendations for convenience stores and human responses to data-driven operational advice.

02 /

AI Governance & Innovation

How should regulators govern products that continuously learn or change after release? My research examines the intersection of AI development, regulatory oversight, innovation incentives, and compliance.

Adaptive AITechnology regulationInnovation incentives

Related work: Regulating adaptive new products and the trade-off between oversight and development practices.

03 /

Behavioral Operations Management

Operational decisions are made by people, not only by models. I examine behavioral biases, social comparisons, feedback, and the interaction between individual decisions and operational outcomes.

Social comparisonInventory decisionsLaboratory experiments

Related work: Inventory sharing, demand-side underweighting, and peer-performance feedback in newsvendor decisions.

04 /

Healthcare, Pharmaceutical & Supply Chain Operations

My work investigates how companies, healthcare systems, and regulators make decisions about innovation, access, incentives, and operational efficiency, alongside data-driven inventory and supply chain management.

Pharmaceutical supply chainsDrug regulationInventory analytics

Related work: Drug reimbursement, accelerated approval pathways, specialty-drug channels, and retail replenishment.
