ABOUT

Research rooted in real-world questions.

I joined Singapore Management University in 2022. Previously, I was an Assistant Professor at the University of Nebraska–Lincoln (2019–2022). I earned my Ph.D. in Supply Chain Management from Penn State University in 2019.

My research draws on data analytics, empirical and experimental methods, game theory, and analytical modeling. I am especially interested in what happens when intelligent algorithms meet human judgment, organizational incentives, and public policy.
